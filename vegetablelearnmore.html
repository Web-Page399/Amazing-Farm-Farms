FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About Our Vegetables

Here are answers to some of the questions our customers frequently ask about our fresh vegetables.

Are your vegetables harvested fresh?

Yes. Our vegetables are harvested fresh from the farm to ensure maximum freshness, taste and nutritional value.

Do you supply vegetables in bulk?

Absolutely. We supply vegetables in both retail and wholesale quantities for supermarkets, restaurants, hotels and food distributors.

Do you deliver outside Ibadan?

Yes. We offer delivery services across Nigeria, depending on the order quantity and destination.

Which vegetables do you produce?

We grow tomatoes, pepper, ugu, spinach, waterleaf, okra, cucumber, onions and other seasonal vegetables.

How do you maintain freshness during delivery?

Our vegetables are carefully sorted, packaged and transported under hygienic conditions to preserve their freshness and quality.

Can restaurants and hotels place regular orders?

Yes. We work with restaurants, hotels, caterers and supermarkets to provide reliable scheduled deliveries.

How can I request a quotation?

Simply contact us through our Contact page, call us, or send us a WhatsApp message. We'll provide pricing based on your order quantity.

Why choose Amazing Farms?

We are committed to producing fresh, high-quality vegetables through responsible farming practices, dependable customer service and consistent product quality.
