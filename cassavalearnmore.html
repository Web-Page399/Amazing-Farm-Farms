Here are answers to some of the most common questions our customers ask about our premium cassava products.

Do you supply cassava in bulk?

Yes. We supply cassava in both retail and wholesale quantities to individuals, distributors, supermarkets, restaurants and food processing companies.

Do you deliver outside Ibadan?

Yes. We can arrange delivery to customers across Nigeria, depending on the quantity ordered and delivery location.

Our cassava is harvested fresh from the farm and carefully handled to maintain its quality before delivery.

Absolutely. We welcome bulk orders from wholesalers, processors, exporters and agricultural businesses.

Yes. We supply healthy, high-quality cassava stems suitable for planting and commercial farming.

We supply fresh cassava tubers, cassava stems, garri, cassava flour and other cassava-based products, depending on seasonal availability.
